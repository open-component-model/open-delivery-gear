## Overview

Open Delivery Gear (ODG) provides automated compliance scanning and tracking for your OCM components. As an end-user, you can:

- **View compliance findings** for your components through the ODG dashboard
- **Track security vulnerabilities** and their remediation status
- **Download SBOMs** (Software Bill of Materials) for your components
- **Monitor SLA compliance** for security findings
